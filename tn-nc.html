About this game

A new adventure in the Toziuha Night series! With a classic 8-bit style and chiptune music, you’ll play through various semi-linear levels to face familiar enemies and new bosses! Whether you play alone or with a friend, the challenge will be waiting for you! However, you’ll also have the option to use modern controls.

You'll be able to revisit areas to find rewards and secrets, and it includes a map to help completionists unlock 100% of the content. Find clues and solve puzzles to help you find a solution to the Nosferatu curse! Inspired by that proto-Metroidvania game released in 1987... Don't let the horrible night fall before you break the curse! As you progress through your adventure, you’ll meet new allies with unique abilities. Be strategic and use the right character at the right time.

Features

  • Classic exploration platformer with 8-bit/chiptune style.
  • Local multiplayer-coop.
  • Classic & Modern Controls.
  • Made with Godot Engine 4.7.
